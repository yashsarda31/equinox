Jockey licensee: volume up, gross margin down

Page Industries fell 13% on 12 November, the day of its Q2 FY27 results, and has drifted 2% since (NIFTY +0.3%). Revenue rose 11.5% to ₹1,440 cr on 5.5% more pieces; profit rose 4.0% to ₹203 cr. Gross margin fell 272bp to 57.2% as purchases of traded goods rose 22% against 10% for own raw material: more of the range is outsourced or imported. First-half profit was flat at ₹395 cr on revenue up 9.7%. Note 9 discloses that the RBI returned the company’s FEMA compounding application on 16 October and wants post-facto government approval first; management expects no material cost.

In H2 FY27E revenue grows 11% to ₹2,930 cr, margin is 21.2% against 21.9%, and EPS rises 7% to ₹377; FY27E EPS of ₹732 is in line with consensus. For FY28E we model revenue up 11% to ₹6,427 cr, a 21.6% margin and EPS up 16% to ₹847, against consensus of ₹6,527 cr and ₹849. Management’s ₹8,000 cr target is for FY28-29, not FY28: it needs 18% growth a year; we model 10-11%. FY29E EPS is ₹960. Every 100bp of FY28E margin is ₹64 cr of EBITDA; capacity of 280 mn pieces against 239 mn annualised means growth needs little new capex.

Our Dec-27 target of ₹27,250 averages 37x the twelve-month EPS after Dec-27 (₹932), or ₹34,476, and a ten-year FCFE DCF at an 11% cost of equity and 6.5% terminal growth plus Dec-27 net cash, or ₹20,013. At ₹31,105 the shares are 37x FY28E and 33x that forward EPS; the DCF needs free cash flow to grow 16.2% a year to FY37, against 8.7% we forecast. The multiple leg alone implies 11%; the cash flow leg is the call. Listed peers trade at 16.7x forward and Page’s own three-year average is 57x, which we do not use as a leg. Consensus of ₹37,659 (26 analysts, 15 buy) is 21% above the price. What would prove us wrong: gross margin above 58% on 12% volume growth, or the ₹8,000 cr target delivered.
