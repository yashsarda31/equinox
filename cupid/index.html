Cupid’s Q2 FY27 headline was strong: revenue of ₹188 cr, up 122% y/y, and net profit of ₹56.2 cr, up 133%. The quality beneath it slipped. Other income included a ₹20.0 cr fair-value gain on investments; before it, pre-tax profit was ₹52.9 cr, 12% below Q1. EBITDA fell 9% q/q to ₹54.8 cr on 21% more revenue, as gross margin dropped 10 points to 48%: bought-in goods were 64% of H1 cost of sales. Cash fell from ₹186 cr in March to ₹17 cr, leaving net debt of ₹90 cr after ₹225 cr of investments and ₹79 cr of capex.

Guidance rose a fourth time, to ₹880 cr of income and ₹275 cr of profit for FY27 and ₹605 cr of profit in FY29, a 30-31% net margin throughout. We accept the FY27 revenue: the Palava plant starts in Q3 and two retail chains are being stocked. We differ on three points. 1) Margin: we assume 26-27% EBITDA against 29% in Q2, as traded goods gain share. 2) V2 Retail: ₹400 cr from 1.7 lakh sq ft is ₹1,960 a sq ft a month, 2.5x V2’s own ₹785; we take 60%. 3) Style Baazar: ₹200 cr is 11% of that chain’s FY26 sales, and Cupid is paying ₹332 cr for c.12% of it. EQe profit is ₹197 cr, ₹211 cr and ₹261 cr for FY27-29E, 28-57% below guidance.

At ₹357 Cupid is valued at ₹48,004 cr: 283x trailing earnings and 79x the profit guided for FY29. Our Dec-27 target of ₹90 applies 47x, the highest forward P/E among ten Indian consumer peers (median 30x), to 12-month-forward EPS of ₹1.85, plus ₹4 of investments. Granting guidance in full, the same multiple gives ₹197, 45% below the price. We rate the shares Underweight (ladder: −5% to +15%). Risks to our view: FY26 guidance was beaten, promoters added 1.7 points to their stake in H1, and with no stock futures momentum can persist.
