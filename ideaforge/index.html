Profit is back; the order book is not

ideaForge has fixed its P&L. Second-quarter revenue more than doubled to ₹91.3cr and profit swung to ₹6.5cr from a ₹19.6cr loss; first-half revenue of ₹159.9cr nearly tripled, with EBITDA of ₹27.5cr against a ₹23.1cr loss. A ₹500cr QIP in July (₹795 a share, 14.5% dilution) left ₹404cr of net cash. The shares are 53% higher over 12 months, 94% above March’s ₹366 low and 29% below June’s peak. The order book has not recovered: it fell from ₹310cr on 1 April to ₹202cr on 30 September on first-half inflow of just ₹52cr.

1) FY27 revenue is ₹370cr — first-half ₹160cr, the ₹202cr backlog and H2 bookings — against consensus ₹367cr. 2) EBITDA margin rises from 11.9% to 23.8%, then 26.5% and 27.5%, below Q4 FY26’s 52.6% but far above FY26. 3) FY28 revenue grows 15% to ₹425cr, 28% below consensus ₹587cr, because it needs ₹420cr of new orders and the ₹20,000cr fast-track pipeline is still at RFP stage. 4) EPS is ₹6.69, ₹8.11 and ₹11.06; consensus FY28 is ₹19.7. Capex was 39% of sales last year and working capital absorbed ₹88cr: free cash flow was −₹152cr in FY26 and −₹102cr this year.

Our Dec-27 target of ₹635 averages two methods that disagree fourfold. At 43x EV/EBITDA — a 21% premium to today’s 35x on FY27E but below the 50x trailing median of Data Patterns, Zen, Solar, BEL and HAL — on FY28E EBITDA of ₹109cr plus net cash, worth ₹1,014. A DCF (12.5% cost of equity, 4.5% terminal growth) charging capex at 9% of sales and 45% of new revenue in working capital gives ₹260. The gap is the case: growth consumes cash. We rate them Underweight (Neutral: −5% to +15%). Upside: fast-track orders land early, a big export win. Downside: inflow undershoots ₹310cr.
