Fast growth; the ‘normalised PAT’ is pre-tax

Rentomojo’s Q2 FY27 results were strong: revenue rose 53% y/y to ₹142.1 cr on 1.05m live items (+44%), and profit before exceptional items and tax rose 52% to ₹24.0 cr. The company calls that figure ‘normalised PAT’, but it is struck before tax. No current tax is paid while past losses last, and the ₹27.5 cr deferred tax asset left at September shelters only about ₹109 cr of profit, used up by mid-FY28E on our forecasts. Taxed at 25.5%, we forecast FY27E EPS of ₹7.44; the shares, up 51% on the ₹404 September IPO price, trade at 82x that.

Three drivers. 1) Fleet: H1 added 198k items net against 217k in all of FY26; we assume 184k in H2, then 430k and 480k in FY28-29E. 2) Price: revenue per item (ARPI) grows 3-5% a year, occupancy holds at 85.5%. 3) Margin: normalised EBITDA has been 40-43% of income since FY24 (35% before lease accounting); we add 100bp by FY29E. Revenue grows 53%, 45% and 35% and EPS compounds at 47%. Growth is bought with capital: H1 capex of ₹192 cr was 1.75x operating cash flow, and we see net debt rising from ₹131 cr to c.₹500 cr by FY29E.

That path is largely in the price. Our Dec-27 target of ₹680 applies 48x to twelve-month forward EPS, the midpoint of today’s 61x and the 35x median of five listed workspace and furniture peers; trailing EV/EBITDA is 33x against their 15x. An FCFE DCF (12% cost of equity, 5.5% terminal growth, 22% return on new equity) gives only ₹277, because growth above ROE absorbs cash. We rate the shares Neutral (ladder: −5% to +15%). Risks: credit and asset losses (3.2% of H1 revenue, before the ₹11.4 cr warehouse fire), share supply from pre-IPO funds (promoters own 19.7%) and funding costs.
