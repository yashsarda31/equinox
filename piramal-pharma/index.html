The turn is real; the price has caught up

Piramal Pharma has broken a run of five weak quarters. Second-quarter revenue rose 28% to ₹2,617cr, with contract manufacturing (CDMO) up 36% and hospital generics up 32%; EBITDA rose 72% to ₹385cr and the margin widened from 11.0% to 14.7%. Profit was ₹5cr against a ₹99cr loss. CDMO added 22 customers in the half and on-patent commercial revenue grew 39%. Management raised FY27 revenue growth guidance from mid-teens to 19-20%. The shares are 42% higher this year, 24% higher in a month and 85% above March’s ₹132 low.

1) FY27 revenue is ₹10,680cr (+20.4%) — first-half ₹4,887cr and a second half of ₹5,793cr, up 18% — against guidance of 19-20%. 2) EBITDA margin rises from 12.8% to 16.2%, then 18.5% and 20.5%; EBITDA compounds at 35% a year to FY29, against 39% at ICICI Securities. 3) Little of it reaches shareholders: on FY28 EBITDA of ₹2,233cr, depreciation takes ₹1,010cr, interest ₹340cr and tax 38%, leaving ₹598cr. 4) EPS is ₹1.70, ₹4.49 and ₹7.41. Net debt rose ₹695cr in six months to ₹4,859cr, 2.8x FY27 EBITDA.

Our Dec-27 target of ₹270 averages two methods that disagree. At 17x Dec-28E EBITDA of ₹2,636cr — today’s multiple on FY28E — less ₹4,500cr of net debt, the shares are worth ₹303. At 35x Dec-28E EPS of ₹6.68, in line with Sun Pharma and Cipla, they are worth ₹234. The gap is the cost of the balance sheet: return on equity is 7% in FY28. Eleven brokers have a mean target of ₹270; the stock trades at 54x FY28E earnings after a 24% month. We rate the shares Neutral (band: −5% to +15%). Upside risks: faster CDMO orders, on-patent launches, Kenalog. Downside: lumpy contracts, renewed destocking, the Bayer exit in December.
