Titan is 10% off its ₹5,169 high (28-Aug), at 54x NTM; the listed jeweller peers are at 22x forward. Q2 FY27: income ₹20,533 cr (+25%, ex-bullion), EBIT ₹2,865 cr (+59%; 14.0%); PAT ₹2,393 cr reported, ₹1,722 cr (+54%) ex the ₹671 cr Damas put-option fair-value credit (non-cash, not earnings). Jewellery fired: studded in the early thirties, tickets double-digit; CaratLane +31%, Watches +27%, International +105% (Damas core ₹364 cr). The ₹536 cr customs-duty gain is inventory profit, not run-rate: ex-gains India jewellery EBIT margin 10.7% vs 11.0% PY — locals with duty-gain firepower are discounting. Stock +-3.9% since results vs NIFTY +2.8%.

Diwali lands in Q3 FY27 (8-Nov); coins tapered (high single-digit decline), plain gold grew ~20%. We model H2 income +17% to ₹52,524 cr at 11.4% EBIT margin: FY27E ₹93,810 cr (+23%), ex-FV EPS ₹82.1 (reported ₹89.7) vs consensus ₹79.1 (19 brokers, ₹70.9–96.5; cons implies H2 EPS flat y/y, we see +32%). FY28E +15%, FY29E +13%, studded-led; jewellery margins anchored ~11% (call, 5-Nov). Watches +27% at 21.9% margin is the second engine; Damas (67%, ₹1,190 cr + ₹1,668 cr option, cut ₹671 cr on GCC weakness) drags now; Tranche-2 post-CY29.

Our Dec-27 target ₹4,160 averages two methods: own 3-yr avg P/E (63.5x; range 44–101x) on 12m-forward EPS ₹104 → ₹6,589; a ten-year FCFE DCF at 12%/5.5% → ₹1,727 (62% terminal; 20% of incremental income to net working capital, gold-loan funded). The legs are ₹4,862 apart — we print both. Consensus mean ₹5,518 (35 brokers; 31 buy/SB, 2 hold, 2 sell; street low ₹4,700): our target is below all 35. Wrong if: studded holds 20%+ into FY28; duty-gain firepower fades restoring 12% margins; Damas GCC recovers; or the WC drag proves FY26-actual (6%). Downside: gold re-rating, studded-mix softness.
