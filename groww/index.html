Growth is intact; the multiple has caught up

Groww’s second quarter looked flat and was not. Revenue was ₹1,502cr, up 47% on the year and unchanged on June, because equity-derivative income fell 14.3% after SEBI’s closing auction began on 3 August. Everything else grew: margin trading was 10.1% of income against 4.7% a year earlier, commodity derivatives gained 6pp, and profit rose 54% to ₹726cr on a 64.2% EBITDA margin. Customer assets reached ₹3.89 lakh crore, up 46%; the AMC’s AUM rose 145% to ₹7,179cr. The shares are 46% higher this year, 13% in a month, and 2.5% below last week’s ₹233 high.

1) FY27 revenue is ₹6,223cr (+34%), against Motilal Oswal’s ₹6,174cr — first-half ₹3,003cr and a second half of ₹3,220cr, up 18%. 2) EBITDA margin holds at 63.8%, then 64.5% and 65.3%, as the cost-to-serve ratio keeps falling. 3) Profit is ₹2,991cr in FY27, ₹3,700cr in FY28 and ₹4,492cr in FY29, a 29% EPS CAGR; ₹3,849cr of cash funds a loan book adding ₹600-700cr a quarter without new debt. 4) EPS is ₹4.77, ₹5.90 and ₹7.16, so the shares trade at 48x FY27E and 39x FY28E.

Our Dec-27 target of ₹250 averages two methods that agree. At 36x Dec-28E EPS of ₹6.84 — the stock’s one-year-forward average since listing and a shade under the 38x Motilal Oswal pays for FY28E — they are worth ₹246. At 27x Dec-28E EBITDA of ₹5,733cr, a shade under today’s 28x on FY28E, plus ₹4,400cr of net cash, they are worth ₹254. Seven brokers average ₹240. We rate the shares Neutral (band: −5% to +15%). Upside risks: CAS relief, the US-stocks launch, MTF share gains. Downside: another derivatives cut, a weaker market, and 53x trailing earnings.
