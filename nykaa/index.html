Beauty drives profit while inventory builds

Nykaa’s Q2 gross merchandise value grew 30% to ₹6,151cr. Revenue rose 28% to ₹3,006cr. EBITDA rose 65% to ₹263cr, lifting the margin from 6.8% to 8.7%, and PAT rose 207% to ₹101cr. Beauty EBITDA grew 51% to ₹267cr. Fashion net sales value rose 47% and its EBITDA margin on that measure reached 0.2%. Management plans to take Nykaa Now from 15 to 25+ cities by March. Inventory absorbed ₹565cr in H1, taking operating cash flow to −₹29cr. Shares are up 37% YTD and 1.2% below their 52-week high.

1) FY27E revenue of ₹12,889cr grows 28.6% versus 28.1% consensus. H2 needs 29% growth, with Q3 at 31% and Q4 at 26%. November’s Pink Friday sale supports Q3 demand. 2) FY27E EBITDA reaches ₹1,204cr at a 9.3% margin. Owned beauty brands and lower marketing costs as a share of sales drive the gain. 3) EBITDA grows 46% annually to FY29. The low end of Nykaa’s FY30 goal still needs 29% growth in FY30. 4) Diluted EPS is ₹1.65 in FY27E, ₹2.64 in FY28E and ₹4.04 in FY29E. FY27E FCF of ₹163cr assumes inventory unwinds and H2 operating cash flow rises from ₹515cr to ₹715cr.

Our Dec-27 target of ₹370 is the rounded average of ₹367 at 49x Dec-28E EBITDA of ₹2,175cr and ₹369 at 100x Dec-28E EPS of ₹3.69. Both multiples sit below today’s FY28E 62x and 138x respectively, allowing for a slower growth rate and quick-commerce spend. The target implies 1.6% upside from ₹364. The broker mean is ₹349. We rate Nykaa Neutral (−5% to +15%). Upside risks include faster adoption of owned brands and higher Fashion margins. Downside risks include heavier discounting and slower inventory turnover.
