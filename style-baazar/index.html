A Q2 loss, ₹175 cr more debt and a 325 MW solar plan

Style Baazar runs 283 value-fashion stores in eastern India. Q2 FY27 results came on Saturday 14 Nov, after Friday’s close at a 52-week high. Revenue fell 10% to ₹478 cr as Durga Puja moved into Q3; that was known. The profit line was not: gross margin fell to 29.1% (30.5%), other expenses rose 21%, EBITDA fell 55% to ₹31 cr and the loss after tax was ₹32 cr, against a ₹51 cr profit that included a ₹41 cr one-off lease gain. H1 same-store sales fell 11.5%. Trailing twelve-month earnings are a ₹37 cr loss; the 73x P/E on data sites is stale.

Cash is the bigger issue. H1 operations used ₹151 cr (+₹109 cr a year ago) as inventory rose ₹151 cr to ₹834 cr (143 days against 108) and other assets ₹132 cr. Cupid has paid in ₹144 cr, of which ₹79 cr repaid loans (CARE), yet borrowings rose from ₹348 cr to ₹522 cr. We assume the pre-Puja stock unwinds by March. On the same day the company gave two FY27 revenue numbers (₹2,301 cr in the deck, ₹2,500 cr in its releases), targeted ₹5,600 cr and 600 stores by FY29 and proposed a 325 MW solar plant. Its ₹30 cr saving and four-year payback imply ₹120 cr; at ₹4 cr per MW (our assumption) it costs ₹1,300 cr, 2.3x net worth.

We forecast Q3 revenue of ₹712 cr (+53%) with Puja and Diwali together, H2 growth of 38% (₹2,500 cr needs 65%) and FY27E net income near nil; the one broker on Yahoo has EPS of ₹8.9. For FY29E we have 479 stores, revenue of ₹3,744 cr and EPS of ₹7.31. Our Dec-27 target of ₹205 is 33x (peer median) forward EPS of ₹6.22; the company’s ₹5,600 cr at our 1.7% margin gives ₹361, a cash-rent DCF ₹54. We rate the shares Underweight. Upside risk: a strong festive Q3. Also note promoter holding fell from 45.7% to 35.1% since March, six directors resigned since August and the company is suing Cupid for ₹28 cr.
