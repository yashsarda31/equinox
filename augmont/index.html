The metal is revenue; the spread is profit

Augmont’s revenue is a gold-price echo; its profit is a spread. FY26 turnover of ₹94,186cr on 53t of gold and 1,000t of silver earned ₹387cr of EBITDA, ₹73 a gram and 0.4% of sales. Q2 revenue fell 27% to ₹15,836cr as rupee gold rose a third, yet EBITDA rose 74% to ₹164cr and margin reached 1.0%, against 0.4% for FY26: the spread widened as the metal moved. The shares, listed at ₹908 on 31 August, are now ₹956 — 21% above the ₹788 issue, 32% below October’s ₹1,405 peak — on gold at $3,889, 27% off January’s high.

1) FY27 revenue is ₹96,182cr, up 2%: H1 ₹34,782cr, H2 ₹61,400cr, 6% above last year’s second half. 2) EBITDA margin is 0.5% against 0.4% in FY26 and 1.0% in Q2; we hold H2 at 0.47%, and every 10bp is ₹96cr — a quarter of last year’s EBITDA. 3) FY28 revenue grows 11% to ₹106,760cr on 55 tonnes, EBITDA ₹582cr, EPS ₹51.64; August’s ₹620cr of fresh money halved ROE from 53% to 29%. 4) Free cash flow was −₹43cr in FY26 and −₹95cr in FY27 as gold absorbed working capital; we model ₹338cr and ₹386cr, 3.9% and 4.4% of market value, on capex of 0.03% of sales.

Our Nov-27 target of ₹1,040, rounded, averages two methods that disagree by 54%. At 18x EV/EBITDA — a 9% premium to today’s 16.6x but below the 20x median of Titan, Kalyan, Thangamayil, Senco, Goldiam and PC Jeweller — FY28E EBITDA of ₹5,818mn plus ₹10,311mn of FY28E net cash is worth ₹1,259. A DCF (11.5% CoE, 5.0% growth, capex 0.03% of sales, 0.5% of new revenue in working capital) gives ₹817. The gap is the case: a half-percent spread compounds only if it widens. We rate the shares Neutral (−5% to +15%). Upside: a 0.62% margin on 15% more revenue. Downside: gold at $3,600.
