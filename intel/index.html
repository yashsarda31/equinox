Down 61% from the June high; the AI CPU is real, the multiple is not

Intel is down 61% from its 22 June close high of $140.94 ($142.35 intraday), and 40% from the $92.07 close on 29 October; the stock fell 29% the next day (YTD +50%; 3-month -47%). At $55.17 the shares trade at 30.8x FY27E consensus non-GAAP EPS, double the peer median (15.4x; NVDA 9.6, TSM 15.4, AVGO 17.6, QCOM 13.7, AMD 28.5); that consensus was cut 16% in 30 days. We rate INTC Underweight, Dec-27 target $30.

Q3 revenue rose 21% YoY to $16.5bn (beats in Q2 and Q3); non-GAAP EPS $0.52 vs $0.38 guided. DCAI — $7.3bn, +78% (ASP +54%, volume +10%), a 42% op margin — against CCPG, down 5% to $8.1bn. Products earned $5.3bn at a 34.5% margin; Foundry lost $1.9bn (9M: $6.4bn). 9M GAAP loss $11.4bn includes $12.6bn non-cash escrow-share MTM and $4.3bn restructuring (Mobileye goodwill). Adjusted FCF was $1.6bn in Q3 ($7.3bn in 9M); cash+ST $54.0bn vs debt $50.1bn. Q4 guidance — revenue $15.0–16.0bn, non-GAAP EPS $0.25 — embeds client revenue down >20% Q/Q and a 2027 PC market down ~10% (call commentary, not independently verifiable).

Our $30 target averages the peer-median forward P/E (15.4x FY27E $1.79 = $28) and a ten-year FCFF DCF at a 12.8% WACC (COE 14.2%; TG 2.5%) of $37; at the CAPM cost of equity on the 1Y beta (WACC 16.9%) it is $25. Even 25x FY27E gives $45, below the price. The street mean of $91 (43 brokers) has not moved despite the 16% FY27E cut; the lowest target is +14% away. The $22.6bn August raise priced at $95 is under water; the 13-November class action (news) alleges the PC slowdown was concealed. What would prove us wrong: an external 14A anchor customer (0.9 PDK landed in Oct), or Xeon constraints persisting as PCs stabilise. Key risk: DCAI beats re-rate it; vol is 91.
