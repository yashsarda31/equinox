Greaves Cotton is up 30.6% since the 29-Oct print (NIFTY: +10.7%) and 28.3% YTD. At ₹244.00 it trades at 73x trailing, 28x Yahoo forward, 4.3x book. Below the headline: revenue +44% to ₹1,176cr, but reported PBT was -₹16cr after ₹32.8cr of exceptions (IPO costs, goodwill, ELE brand) + ₹27cr accelerated D&A (Teja brand, tools). Core EBITDA margin compressed to 11.5% from 14.8% on RMC inflation; pricing actions flow through Q3-Q4.

1) H1: core revenue +20% to ₹1,478cr (EBITDA 178cr, 12.0% vs 14.9%); GEML revenue +103% to ₹680cr; FY27 Ampere VAHAN registrations (to 15-Nov) 70,333 > FY26's 61,842; 5th position, 5.7% share (Nov). GEML's EBITDA loss narrowed to ₹63cr (₹78cr); breakeven expected soon; 40k units/month capacity at ~40% util. 2) Our FY27E: revenue ₹4,588cr (+34%; H2 +30% YoY on pricing), owners' PAT ₹85cr, EPS ₹3.65. FY28E ₹6.44 (+76%), FY29E ₹9.23 (+43%). 3) Balance: FY26 revenue ₹3,437cr (+18%), business PBT ₹154cr (+118%); core net cash ₹110cr; ₹331cr infused into GEML's ₹530cr rights issue (funds 12-18 months); Excel Controlinkage now 100% owned; GFL AUM ₹630cr (+65% YoY).

Our Dec-27 target of ₹230 averages two methods. Peer-median forward P/E of 32.5x (Cummins 37.8 / Kirloskar Oil 30.4 / Thermax 34.4 / Escorts 18.5) on forward-blended EPS ₹8.53 gives ₹277. A ten-year FCFE DCF (PAT + D&A - capex - incremental WC; 12% COE, 5.5% TG) gives ₹191. The gap is the argument: GEML is unprofitable, IPO decision shelved (committee, no date), and its FY28 revenue ramp does the work in both legs. At ₹244, -5.7% is Neutral. What would prove us wrong: GEML reaching EBITDA breakeven through FY27-28 (an IPO or spin-off, once decided, would re-rate it); pricing landing in Q3/Q4 lifting core margins back to 13%+.
