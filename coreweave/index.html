CoreWeave

Can the backlog pay for the buildout?

CoreWeave’s third-quarter revenue rose 168% to $3.66bn as active power passed 1.7 GW. CoreWeave can recognize revenue from its $132.5bn backlog as it delivers computing capacity. Two customers supplied 54% of quarterly sales. Operating income reached $207m, but net interest expense of $846m pushed the quarter to a $709m net loss. Through September, cash from operations covered $5.64bn of $23.56bn in cash purchases of property and equipment. The shares closed at $49.89, down 30% this year.

1) We forecast $4.84bn of Q4 revenue, up 32% sequentially, to reach the $13.15bn midpoint of FY26 guidance. The adjusted operating profit midpoint requires a 16.5% Q4 margin, up from 10.4% in Q3. 2) Our FY27 sales of $25.5bn assume quarterly revenue rises from $5.25bn to $7.50bn as new sites begin billing. 3) We hold FY27 capital spending at $38bn. Management expects $9bn in customer deposits and prepayments over three months, reducing its near-term borrowing needs. 4) We model $4.8bn of FY27 net interest against $3.2bn of operating income, leaving a $2.93 loss per share.

Our Dec-27 target of $59 averages two methods. Holding today’s 2.8x forward sales multiple on $35.5bn of FY28 revenue gives $57 per share. A DCF gives $61 at an 11% cost of capital and 3.5% terminal growth. Both deduct $66bn of Dec-27 net debt and assume 590m shares. Each additional $5bn of net debt costs $8.47 per share if enterprise value holds. The target offers 18% upside, above our 15% Overweight threshold. Faster site openings or cheaper debt lift equity value. Power delays or a customer deferral would leave interest accruing ahead of revenue.
