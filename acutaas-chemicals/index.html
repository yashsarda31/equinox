The print is real; the price is ahead

Acutaas Chemicals has repriced itself. September-quarter revenue rose 42.7% to ₹436.9cr and EBITDA 84.6% to ₹176.0cr, a 40.3% margin against 31.1%; first-half revenue of ₹766.6cr was up 49.3% and profit ₹197.4cr, up 70%. Capex of ₹300cr this year is funded from ₹300.9cr of net cash. The shares are 140% higher over 12 months, 157% above January’s ₹1,570 low and 2.2% below Friday’s peak. The specialty chemicals segment went the other way: revenue fell 20% to ₹34.9cr on phased-out commodity products and a paused US shipment.

1) FY27 revenue is ₹1,728cr, the 30% management guides rather than the 41% consensus assumes: first-half ₹767cr, second-half ₹961cr. 2) EBITDA margin holds at 38.5%, inside the 38-40% guide and below Q2’s 40.3%. 3) FY28 revenue grows 22% to ₹2,100cr, 14% below consensus ₹2,449cr, and needs the Korean plant to qualify and specialty chemicals to stop shrinking. 4) EPS is ₹57.06, ₹71.02 and ₹86.46; consensus FY28 is ₹81.5. Capex runs at 17% of sales and working capital at 115 days: free cash flow is ₹96cr in FY27 and ₹267cr in FY28.

Our Dec-27 target of ₹3,125 averages two methods that disagree 2.5-fold. At 44x EV/EBITDA — an 11% discount to today’s 49x on FY27E, above the 39x trailing median of Divis, Laurus, Neuland, SRF and Navin — on FY28E EBITDA of ₹830cr plus net cash, worth ₹4,481. A DCF (11.5% cost of equity, 4.5% terminal) charging capex at 8% of sales and 22% of new revenue in working capital gives ₹1,766. The gap is the case: the price capitalises a decade. We rate them Underweight: −22.6% vs a −5% band. Upside: CDMO converts early, Korea scales. Downside: consensus revenue is 11 points too high.
