MV Electrosystems listed on 6-Aug-2026 (100% fresh, ₹425; ₹290cr; face ₹5); up 33% since listing, down 12% over 1 month, ~15% below the ₹978 peak. At ₹827, FY26 earnings are negative (rev ₹49.4cr, PAT ₹-12.6cr, op CF ₹−57.5cr); auditors flagged quarterly cash losses (₹61.8m Q2FY27; ₹136.8m FY26) in an emphasis of matter, with a going-concern note assessed by management. No analyst covers it. Proxy peer: Hirect (approvals pending).

1) FY26 broke on build-ahead (45 R&D hires vs billing): rev ₹49.4cr (−21%), EBITDA ₹-10.3cr, PAT ₹-12.6cr. 2) Setup flipped: ₹1,090cr book as of 30-Sep-26 — 557 sets, ₹975.6cr incl. 3-yr AMC + developmental ₹94.7cr + other ~₹20cr. CLW cleared commercial supply after 50,000 km of trials; 7 sets delivered Sep-26; deck: ~50 sets by end-Q3FY27, then ~40/month (cap 55/month). 3) H1 FY27: rev ₹31.7cr (+22%), PAT ₹-12.1cr vs ₹−2.9cr H1FY26; cash ₹197.6cr (₹194.4cr of the ₹290cr IPO unutilized); net cash ₹141.4cr; H1 op CF −₹63.7cr.

Our Dec-27 target ₹540 averages two methods. Cohort fwd P/E median 35.5x (Havells 36.7 / Polycab 29.6 / Kaynes 36.6 / APAR 34.8) on forward-blended EQe EPS ₹19.61 (0.25 FY28E ₹10.26 + 0.75 FY29E ₹22.73) ⇒ ₹696. A 10-yr FCFE DCF (13% COE, 5.5% TG) ⇒ ₹375 (PV ₹238cr + TV ₹643cr + net cash ₹141cr). The gap to ₹827 is the argument — even the cohort leg fails to clear the price: an unproven, loss-making rail-electrification microcap at 7.25x post-IPO book, no coverage. Underweight. What proves us wrong: 150+ sets FY27 and FY28 revenue > ₹500cr (management '10%+ PAT at run-rate', Aug-26 call); then FY29E EPS ₹22.73 × 35.5x ≈ ₹807. Key risks: single-rail concentration (CLW/DLM/ICF); IGBT supply; Vande Bharat approval; cash burn.
