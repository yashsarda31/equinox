Eternal (Zomato)

High growth, even higher price?

Eternal has gained 16% since its Q2 FY27 results on 21 October and is 2% off its 52-week high. The results earned it: B2C NOV grew 54%, Blinkit’s 79%, and Adjusted EBITDA tripled to ₹666 cr. But that measure stops before stock pay, depreciation and tax. After ₹262 cr of stock pay and ₹432 cr of depreciation and lease charges, the business lost ₹28 cr before ₹351 cr of treasury income. Tax took 61% of pre-tax profit: food’s profit is taxed in the parent; losses elsewhere sit in subsidiaries. Net profit: ₹125 cr. Market value: ₹3.5 lakh cr.

1) Food NOV grows 21%, 19% and 18% in FY27-29E at a flat 5.5% margin. 2) Blinkit NOV grows 82%, 55% and 42%, to ₹1.94 lakh cr in FY29E (guidance: ₹1.99 lakh cr), on 5,000 stores by March 2028; margin rises from 0.9% to 3.0% in FY29E and 5.5% by FY34E (guidance: 5-6%). 3) Adjusted EBITDA reaches ₹9,835 cr in FY29E, above the company’s US$1bn aim. Yet our EPS is 38% and 29% below consensus for FY27E and FY28E: stores cost ₹2.5 cr each, so capex is ₹3,864 cr this year, depreciation follows, and the parent pays full tax (FY27E rate: 56%).

Our Dec-27 target of ₹305 averages the two methods used for Swiggy. A 37x P/E, midway between the medians of Indian internet and consumer names (52x) and global delivery platforms (22x), on fully-taxed FY31E EPS of ₹12.4, discounted at 12%, gives ₹355. A DCF to FY36E plus cash gives ₹255. Blinkit decides the answer: at a long-run margin of 4.5% of NOV the target is ₹260, at 6.5% ₹350. We use 5.5%; the price implies 6.8%, above the top of guidance, on NOV that grows 13-fold in ten years. Consensus target: ₹384. Upside: Blinkit margins above 6%; dark.ai. Downside: a five-to-seven-player quick-commerce market; new food entrants.
