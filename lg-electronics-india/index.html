LG Electronics India

Back to FY25 earnings, at 52x

LG Electronics India has slipped 3% since its Q2 FY27 results on 5 November while the NIFTY rose 5%, yet at ₹1,732 it still trades at 52x FY27 consensus EPS. That consensus, ₹33.23, is only 2% above the ₹32.46 earned in FY25: profit fell 24% in FY26 as a weaker rupee, dearer commodities and channel spending cut the EBITDA margin to 9.8% from 12.8%. Q2 itself was good: revenue grew 20% to ₹7,380 cr and net profit 26% to ₹489 cr. But cash from operations was ₹164 cr negative in the quarter as dealers were stocked for the festive season, and ₹870 cr went into the new Sri City plant.

1) In H2 FY27E revenue grows 15%, in line with consensus and mid-teens guidance, and net profit 24%, close to H1’s 26.5%. Consensus needs H2 profit up 42%. Our FY27E EPS of ₹31.07 is 7% below it, on an EBITDA margin of 10.6%, inside the “early double-digit” guide. 2) Sri City starts compressors in Q3 FY27 and air conditioners in Q4, doubling capacity; appliance EBIT margin rises to 10.8% in FY28E and EPS grows 17%. 3) The ₹5,000 cr plant absorbs the cash: free cash flow is negative in FY27E and net cash stays near ₹4,470 cr.

Our Dec-27 target of ₹1,110 averages two methods. The peer-median forward P/E of 38x (Voltas, Blue Star, Havells, Whirlpool, Crompton), on earnings twelve months ahead, gives ₹1,577. A ten-year DCF at a 12% cost of equity, plus net cash, gives ₹634 (₹740 at 11%). The legs are far apart; at ₹1,732 the shares need free cash flow to grow 31% a year to FY37E, against our 12%; consensus targets ₹1,955. What would prove us wrong: margins back above FY25’s as compressors are made in-house, or exports scaling faster. Risks: a hot summer in Q4, when LG sells about 55% of its air conditioners; a DRI notice seeking ₹154 cr of customs duty on royalty payments.
