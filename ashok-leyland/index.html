Neutral, Dec-27 target ₹165. Q2 FY27 (out on Saturday 14 November) brought record volumes of 64,677 vehicles (+32%) and revenue up 39% to ₹13,364 cr, but EBITDA rose only 16%: the margin fell to 10.1% from 12.1% as materials took 74.9% of revenue against 71.2%. The shares are 1.8% lower since (NIFTY +1.5%) and 22% below February’s high, yet the price still assumes recovery: on cash flow alone it needs a 14.8% EBITDA margin through the cycle, above FY26’s record 13.0%.

1) Management called Q2 the peak for input costs and has raised truck prices about 2.25% since April. We have the H2 margin at 12.6% (14.0% a year ago) on revenue up 11% against a base the GST cut lifted; October domestic volumes rose 24%. FY27E EPS of ₹6.60 is 3% above consensus, which has H2 EPS down 6%. 2) H1 operating cash flow was ₹87 cr negative as payables unwound. 3) The auditors disclaimed their conclusion on the consolidated results: Hinduja Leyland Finance (HLF), the 61%-owned lender with ₹70,403 cr of assets, cannot reconcile loan balances across its systems. FY28E EPS grows 24% as the margin returns to 12.8%.

The target adds two parts. The truck business averages its own three-year P/E of 20.5x on Dec-27 forward EPS (₹180) and a ten-year DCF at 12% with the margin settling at 12%, plus net cash (₹127); the DCF is lower because it does not assume FY26’s peak margin lasts. HLF counts at one times book (₹9 a share); it merges into listed NDL Ventures, leaving Ashok Leyland 59.6%. Other subsidiaries count at nil. What would prove us wrong: a Q4 margin back above 14%, or NDL valued well above book (1.5 times adds ₹5). Risks: H2 demand against the GST-led base; a write-down at HLF after its systems review.
