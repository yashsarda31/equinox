A record quarter, already in the price

PVR INOX is up 18% since Q2 results on 28 October (NIFTY 11%) and 68% this year, a 52-week high. The quarter was its biggest: income ₹2,133 cr, up 17%; EBITDA ₹448 cr before other income (₹475 cr as the company reports it), up 44%; net profit ₹273 cr, up 111%. Admissions rose only 4%, to 46.3m. ATP ₹298 and spend per head ₹153 rose 14%, occupancy reached 30.4% against 28.7%, and 51 paise of every extra half-year rupee reached EBITDA, lifting H1's margin to 17.6% from 12.7%. Other income of ₹28 cr from reversed provisions and a ₹21 cr cut to depreciation flattered it; neither explains a 21.3% margin against 17.3%.

We model H2 income up 14% at a 17.4% margin, below H1's 17.6% and Q2's 21.3%, giving FY27E EBITDA of ₹1,333 cr (FY26: ₹872 cr) and EPS of ₹74.30. That is 38% above consensus of ₹53.69, which implies H2 net profit of ₹172 cr, a 4.5% margin and less than Q2 alone: the screen predates the results. Screens grow from 1,797 to 1,868 by March and 2,033 a year later, mostly asset-light builds; we grow revenue 11% and 10% and hold the margin flat. Capex of ₹310 cr against ₹1,333 cr of EBITDA turns September's ₹141 cr of net cash into ₹1,101 cr by March 2028.

Our target of ₹1,730 averages two methods. Five listed exhibitors trade at a median 10.3x EV/EBITDA (CNK 9.4x, MCS 9.5x, CGX 10.3x, AMC 12.2x, IMAX 14.2x), giving ₹1,638 on the next twelve months' EBITDA. A ten-year DCF at a 12% cost of equity and 5.5% terminal growth gives ₹1,827; at 11-13% the range is ₹2,145-1,594. The legs bracket the price: the multiple says the market already pays for the recovery; the price needs 8% annual free-cash-flow growth to FY37, against 9% forecast. Consensus targets average ₹1,688, below the price; 17 of 18 ratings are buy. Q3 holding Q2's margin, or a screen pipeline earning its cost of capital, would change our mind.
