Pricing power is proven; the price reflects it

Leela Palaces has posted two quarters of 35%+ EBITDA growth. Second-quarter operating revenue rose 33% to ₹412cr, with rooms up 39% and management fees up 76%; RevPAR rose 30% to ₹17,225, all of it room rate, helped by the BRICS summit. Operating EBITDA rose 36% to ₹188cr at a 46% margin and profit rose 27% to ₹95cr. Net debt is ₹1,215cr, 1.4x EBITDA, against 3.7x in March 2025. Management guides to 17-19% EBITDA growth in the second half. The shares are 61% higher this year, 12% higher in a month and 1% below the ₹707 high.

1) FY27 revenue is ₹1,890cr (+23.7%) — first-half ₹764cr and a second half of ₹1,126cr, up 20%. 2) EBITDA is ₹937cr (+26%); our second half grows 20% against the 17-19% guided, on a March quarter hit by the West Asia conflict. 3) EBITDA compounds at 20% a year to ₹1,281cr in FY29; the company’s ambition of ten times FY20 EBITDA by FY30, about ₹1,930cr, needs 24% a year from FY26. 4) EPS is ₹14.98, ₹18.05 and ₹20.86; joint-venture losses, mainly Dubai, were ₹27cr in the first half. Return on equity is 7.5% in FY27 and 8.9% in FY29.

Our Dec-27 target of ₹720 averages two methods that agree. At 21x Dec-28E EBITDA of ₹1,235cr — the stock trades at 22x FY28E, the multiple Elara uses — less ₹1,250cr of net debt, the shares are worth ₹739. At 35x Dec-28E EPS of ₹20.16, between EIH and Chalet at 28-30x trailing and ITC Hotels at 39x, they are worth ₹706. 20 brokers have a mean target of ₹713; the price is within 2% of it after a 63% year. We rate the shares Neutral (band: −5% to +15%). Upside risks: corporate rate resets, acquisitions, BKC Mumbai. Downside: a travel shock, overruns on ₹1,432cr of projects, Dubai losses.
