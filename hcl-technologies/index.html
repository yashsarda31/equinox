HCL Technologies

Margins are mending; growth is not

HCLTech’s second quarter was its best for profit. Revenue rose 11.5% to ₹35,620cr ($3,724m), 2.6% in constant currency; EBIT margin widened 131bp to 18.2%, or 19.0% before 84bp of restructuring, and net profit rose 21% to a record ₹5,132cr. New bookings were $3.3bn, up 27%. Advanced AI revenue reached $219m, up 78%, while software fell 11% as multi-year licence revenue halved. Management narrowed FY27 growth guidance to 2.5-4.0% (top end cut from 4.5%) and held the 17.5-18.5% margin band. The shares are down 17% this year and 24% below February’s ₹1,780 high, though 31% above the low.

1) FY27 revenue is ₹144,749cr (+11.2%), about 3.5% in constant currency, the upper half of guidance; the second half needs ₹74,550cr, 6.2% above the first. 2) EBIT margin is 18.1% against 17.2% in FY26, then 18.5% and 18.7%, as Project Ascend and AI mix offset acquisition dilution. 3) Growth of 7.5-8% in FY28-29 rests on the record bookings, which management says start billing in Q1 FY28. 4) EPS is ₹75.45, ₹80.66 and ₹88.23; FY27’s 23% jump flatters, as FY26 carried a labour-code charge. Net cash is ₹24,503cr, 7% of market value.

Our Dec-27 target of ₹1,520 averages two methods 7% apart. At 17x Dec-28E EPS of ₹86.33, close to today’s 17.8x FY27E and above TCS (16x) and Infosys (14x) trailing, the shares are worth ₹1,468. At 13x Dec-28E EBIT of ₹30,766cr — today’s multiple — plus ₹27,500cr of net cash, they are worth ₹1,575. The 40 brokers we track have a mean target of ₹1,280, below the price. The 4% dividend yield limits the downside; 8% EPS growth limits the upside. We rate the shares Neutral (band: −5% to +15%). Upside risks: faster mega-deal ramp, AI pricing, software renewals. Downside: AI deflation in services, telco and manufacturing cuts, a stronger rupee.
