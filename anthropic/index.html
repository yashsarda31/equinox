IPO initiation: real growth, rich price

Anthropic priced its IPO at $83 on 18 November, above the $70-78 range, selling 956m new shares for $79.4bn beside a $26.0bn placement with Google, NVIDIA and five other suppliers. Trading starts today. On 18.14bn fully diluted shares the offer values the equity at $1.51tn and, net of $232bn of cash, the business at $1.27tn: 15.7x the $81bn run-rate revenue of 31 October and 10.2x our 2027E revenue. We start at Underweight, Dec-27 target $55. On our margins and method, $83 needs 2030E revenue of $474bn; we forecast $287bn.

The growth is real: quarterly revenue rose from $1.4bn to $17.0bn in a year, and customers paying over $100,000 from 950 to 11,900. Compute is the limit. Q3 adjusted operating margin fell to 15% from 19% as $5.0bn of premium-priced short-term compute lifted compute to 58% of revenue (Q2: 48%) and price cuts cost $600m. Commitments total $901bn, $452bn before 2031. We assume run-rate revenue adds $4bn a month to December and $6bn a month in 2027 (October added $10bn): revenue of $53.7bn in 2026E and $125bn in 2027E, then growth of 45%, 30% and 22% to 2030E, with adjusted operating margin reaching 30% and stock pay on new grants at 5-7% of revenue.

Our target averages two methods: 17.9x, the median forward P/E of six AI-platform peers, on 2030E earnings plus cash gives $59; a DCF at an 11.8% cost of equity gives $53. Our bull case (2030E revenue $370bn, 34% margin) gives $77, still below the offer. We would be wrong if revenue grows 56% a year from 2027E to 2030E (we model 32%) or adjusted margin reaches 44% (we model 30%). Other risks: a trust elects most of the board and the co-founders hold 50.1% of votes; the Department of War’s supply-chain designation stands after a 2-1 appeal ruling; 3.7bn shares can be sold before the 180-day lock-up ends.
