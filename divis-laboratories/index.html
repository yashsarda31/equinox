Growth is real, so is the price

Divi’s Laboratories (DIVISLAB) has risen 11% since Saturday’s Q2 FY27 results and 74% over 12m, near a record. Revenue from operations grew 35% to ₹3,676 cr, EBITDA rose 76% to ₹1,566 cr (42.6%) and PAT rose 64% to ₹1,127 cr. H1 revenue was ₹6,756 cr (+31.8% y/y) with EBITDA of ₹2,828 cr (41.9%). Constant-currency sales grew 17%; custom synthesis was 62% of H1 revenue and materials fell to 32% of sales from 40%.

1) We model H2 FY27 revenue up 22% (guidance: double-digit) and EPS of ₹65.91, taking FY27E EPS to ₹142.34, 6% below consensus, which needs H2 profit up 40% y/y. 2) FY28E/FY29E EPS are ₹170.02/₹198.71 as the three dedicated capex programs reach commercial supply; FY27-29E capex is about ₹2,000 cr a year (H1: ₹872 cr; CWIP ₹1,638 cr). 3) Nutraceuticals grew to ₹571 cr from ₹492 cr in H1; working capital is the swing factor (H1 CFO: ₹384 cr).

Our Dec-27 target of ₹6,690 averages two methods. Own three-year average P/E of 53.5x on earnings twelve months after Dec-27 gives ₹10,247. A ten-year DCF of FY28-37 cash flows at 12%, plus Dec-27 net cash of ₹3,977 cr, gives ₹3,141; it is punitive because capex absorbs cash. At ₹11,442 the shares sit at 60x Dec-27-basis earnings; peer CRDMO names trade at 44.0x forward earnings. The consensus target of ₹11,083 is also below price, though 18 of 28 brokers say buy. Proof we are wrong: validation converts to commercial orders and margins stay above 40%. Downside: approvals slip or working capital does not normalise.
