Antelopus Selan Energy (upstream E&P — Bakrol, Lohar, Karjisan in Gujarat’s Cambay basin; Dangeru, Assam; new KG/Cambay acreage) is down 25% since its Q2 results on 26 October (after tripling from ₹357). The quarter wasn’t the trigger: H1 revenue rose 137% to ₹250 cr, EBITDA ₹183 cr (H1FY26: ₹68 cr), profit ₹112 cr, on Brent at $94 (FY26: $69); production 1,656 boepd (Oct: ~1,950). Bakrol’s frac program is complete; Karjisan ahead of plan (2 of 7 drilled; all 7 by April 2027); guide: 5,000+ onshore boepd by FY29.

1) FY27E revenue is ₹574 cr (+106%; H2E ₹324 cr, +87%) on ~1,930 boepd and $91 Brent; FY28E/29E revenue ₹839/1,083 cr as the FY27/28/29 exits (2,500/3,500/5,000 boepd) ramp. EPS ₹67.81 (FY27E, +167%), ₹95.71 (FY28E), ₹118.97 (FY29E). Capex is ₹280 cr FY27 (₹137 cr in H1); the balance sheet stays net cash. 2) Crude is the swing factor ($10/bbl = ~₹50 cr of EBITDA); Karjisan payback is under a year; KG DSF-IV production from FY28. 3) H1 tax 23.7% of PBT; we normalise at 25%. The ₹10 cr Elao CWIP write-off (DGH pending) sits in Q1.

Our Dec-27 target of ₹840 averages two methods. The peer leg — median EV/EBITDA of the three Indian listed E&P companies (ONGC 4.4x, OIL 6.1x, Hindustan Oil Exploration 24.1x) on FY27E EBITDA, plus net cash — gives ₹673. A ten-year DCF at a 12% cost of equity (Brent $75 flat from FY30; USDINR +2.5%/yr) gives ₹1,006; at $90 flat crude, ₹1,268. At ₹887 the shares trade at 13.1x FY27E earnings (17.6x trailing, 10.8x TTM EV/EBITDA), FCF turns positive only in FY29 and the FY29 plan is priced in; Blackbuck Energy Investments owns 71.5%. What would prove us wrong: Karjisan >1,000 boepd and KG first oil on schedule; Brent >$95. Risks: crude normalisation, capex overruns, DGH approvals, 72% volatility, ~29% float.
