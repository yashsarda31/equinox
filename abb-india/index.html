Orders strong, multiple breathless

ABB India shares are up 46% over 12m and 39% YTD, sitting just below the ₹7,925 traded high. Q3 CY26 (Jul-Sep) revenue grew 19% to ₹3,738 cr, Op EBITA rose 15% to ₹484 cr and continuing PAT rose 10% to ₹440 cr. Reported profit, including discontinued robotics operations, was ₹443 cr. Results were announced on Friday after the close, so the 13-Nov price has not yet absorbed them. At ₹7,054, ABB trades at 89x CY26E continuing EPS and 52x CY28E EPS.

1) Demand is extraordinary: record 9M CY26 orders of ₹13,376 cr (+43% y/y), including ₹4,733 cr in Q3 (+56%, a quarterly record) — data centres, utilities, metals and mining, food & beverage, cement and buildings and infrastructure. 2) Mix matters: Electrification delivered record orders of ₹2,780 cr (+117%) and revenue of ₹1,927 cr (+40%); Motion orders ₹1,477 cr (+15%), revenue ₹1,336 cr (+14%); Automation orders ₹499 cr (+4%), revenue ₹505 cr (-16%). 3) The swing factor is profitability: 9M EBITDA margin was 13.5% (vs 15.9% a year ago) and Q3 margin 14.8%; per management, pressures were commodity and logistics costs, currency volatility, pricing pressure and lower volumes, not one-offs. Price hikes can improve margins, but the market already expects execution.

Our target is the average of two explicit legs: 60.0x CY28E EQe EPS (₹8,183) and the published mean (₹7,035), producing ₹7,610 (+7.9%; Neutral). The forward assumptions are aligned to Motilal Oswal's 14-Nov CY26–28 recovery outlook; the target and rating are Equinox's. ABB deserves scarcity economics — 75%-owned by ABB Ltd, ₹12,927 cr backlog, capex announcement of USD 75m and secular electrification/data-centre demand. Motilal is Buy (₹8,200), while Yahoo shows 7 Buy, 8 Hold and 12 Sell/Strong Sell. This is not a short — visibility is real. It is also not a free lunch at 89x CY26E EPS; margin recovery must show up, not merely remain promised.
