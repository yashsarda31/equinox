GNG Electronics

The results held; the memory trade did not

GNG Electronics, India’s largest laptop refurbisher, had its best quarter: revenue rose 42% to ₹625cr, gross margin rose from 19.9% to 25.1% and EBITDA doubled to ₹94cr, a 15.0% margin. Profit rose 72% to ₹56cr. GNG raised laptop prices 11% to ₹31,000; new laptops in India cost 80% more than last year as AI servers absorb memory chips. FY27 guidance rose from 30% to 40% revenue growth and from 100bp to 150bp of PAT margin expansion. The shares hit ₹776, then fell 20% on 6 November as US memory stocks sold off. At ₹485 they are 37% off the high, 58% up this year.

1) FY27 revenue is ₹2,617cr (+38%): first-half ₹1,037cr and a second half up 39%, against guidance of 40%. 2) EBITDA margin rises from 10.6% to 13.7%, then eases to 13.5% and 13.0%: 125-150bp of the Q2 gain was inventory bought before prices rose, and memory supply loosens in 2028. EBITDA grows 39% a year to FY29 (Motilal Oswal: 47%). 3) Debt funds the growth: inventory is ₹912cr, 180 days of cost of sales, borrowings rose ₹205cr in six months and quarterly finance cost is up 2.6x to ₹22cr. Free cash flow turns positive in FY29. 4) EPS is ₹19.19, ₹25.22 and ₹31.09; FY28 is 4% below consensus.

Our Dec-27 target of ₹600 averages two methods that disagree. At 22x Dec-28E EPS of ₹29.62, below Motilal Oswal’s 30x as free cash flow is negative, the shares are worth ₹652. At 13x Dec-28E EBITDA of ₹518cr — today’s multiple on FY28E — less ₹530cr of net debt, they are worth ₹544. The gap is interest, 21% of FY28 EBITDA. The stock trades at 19x FY28E. We rate the shares Overweight (band: above +15%). Upside risks: tight memory into 2028, exports, leaner inventory. Downside: cheaper memory, write-downs, promoter sales (78.7% to 71.8%).
