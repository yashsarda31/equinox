Swiggy

Food pays the bills; Instamart sets the price

Swiggy has lost 17% since its Q2 FY27 results on 22 October and is 52% below its ₹390 IPO price. Food was not the problem: GOV grew 17.7% and Adjusted EBITDA hit a record ₹341 cr, 3.4% of GOV. Instamart was. After contribution breakeven in May it spent to restart growth: contribution margin fell to −1.0% and the loss rose to ₹895 cr. Toing, a budget-meals app, took Platform Innovations’ loss to ₹162 cr from ₹46 cr a year ago. Blinkit grew NOV 79% to ₹20,940 cr, 3.2 times Instamart’s, with Adjusted EBITDA of ₹184 cr.

1) Food GOV grows 18%, 17% and 16% in FY27-29E; margin reaches 4.3% (guidance: 5%). 2) Instamart NOV grows 36%, 29% and 26%; contribution margin is zero by Q4, 1.8% in FY28E and 3.0% in FY29E, with c.80bp from owning inventory. 3) Costs below contribution, ₹803 cr in Q2, grow 0.4 times as fast as GOV, as they have for five quarters. Adjusted EBITDA turns positive in FY30E; Instamart breaks even in FY31E. Our FY31E Adjusted EBITDA is ₹3,629 cr against the ₹10,000 cr target, and EPS ₹9 against ₹30-33. Adjusted EBITDA excludes stock pay: near zero in FY29E, yet the net loss is ₹1,102 cr. Cash of ₹13,662 cr troughs at ₹9,040 cr: no new equity needed.

Our Dec-27 target of ₹210 averages two methods. A 37x P/E, midway between the 52x median of listed Indian internet and consumer names and the 22x of global delivery platforms, on fully-taxed FY31E EPS of ₹6.7, discounted at 12%, plus ₹8 for tax losses, gives ₹200. A DCF to FY36E (5.5% terminal growth) plus cash gives ₹220. Instamart sets the answer: at a long-run margin of 1.5% of GOV the same method gives ₹120, at 4.5% ₹300. We use 3.0%; the price implies 2.6%. The consensus mean target is ₹303. We rate the shares Neutral (ladder: −5% to +15%). Upside: Blinkit-like margins, Toing widening the food market. Downside: e-commerce majors in quick commerce, Rapido in food.
