Anupam Rasayan is up 13% since the 6-Nov print (NIFTY: +1.4%), +21% 12 months, but down 5% off its May peak ₹1,376. At ₹1,316, 87x trailing as-filed earnings (₹15.09) and 1.9x book; consensus: 7 ratings, (4 SB, 1 Buy, 2 Sell), mean ₹1,263. Q2 FY27 (6-Nov): revenue ₹821cr (+11%), EBITDA ₹202cr (+41%, 25% margin), PAT ₹65cr (+14%), owners ₹50.0cr (+13%). EPS restated same day to owners' basis: ₹4.39 Q2 / ₹7.79 H1 (Excel-formula slip; earlier print ₹6.81 / ₹10.21).

1) Organic is a base effect: standalone H1 ₹726cr vs ₹904cr (-20% after a one-time ₹180cr sale in Q2FY26); consolidated H1 ₹1,489cr (+21%), EBITDA ₹377cr (+38%; margin 25.5% vs 22.2%), owners PAT ₹88.7cr (+13%). Q2 included 3 days of Bliss (28–30 Sep); Jayhawk and Tanfac fully in H1. 2) Our FY27E: revenue ₹3,161cr (+34%), EBITDA ₹760cr, owners PAT ₹194cr, EPS ₹17.04. FY28E ₹23.72 (+39%), FY29E ₹28.99 (+22%). 3) FY26: total income ₹2,384cr (+65%), PAT ₹222cr (+39%), op CF ₹334cr; net debt ₹1,765cr (incl ₹300cr Bliss term loan); order book ~₹17,500cr (6-Nov call); Bliss 50.07% closed 28-Sep; Tanfac ₹61cr preferential; 6-yr LTSA (US specialty metal).

Our Dec-27 target ₹1,110 averages two methods. Our own 3-yr average P/E of 72x on forward-blended EPS ₹27.67 gives ₹1,992. An FCFE DCF (12% COE, 5.5% TG) gives ₹228 (explicit PV ₹1,834cr + TV ₹2,529cr, less net debt ₹1,765cr). Own-history P/E is a regime average; every cash-flow lens says the price outruns the ramp. At ₹1,316, -15.7% is Underweight. What would prove us wrong: Bliss + Tanfac + Jayhawk delivering ₹270cr+ owners PAT in FY28 (our EQe) (our FY28E ₹23.72/share; Yahoo on 3 estimates ₹41.1); conversion of the geological-resources / specialty-metal LTSA; an FY28 EPS upgrade. Key risks: leverage, NCI drag, Bliss integration.
