How a note is built

01

A dated price

Every publication states the close it is priced at, and whether results landed after it. Implied moves on this site are measured from that price, not today’s.

02

Both valuation legs

Targets usually average a multiple-based value and a cash-flow value. Both legs are printed, so a reader can see when one of them is doing the work.

03

The one-off named

Each note looks inside the latest result for what will not repeat — a tax credit, a stake sale, a fair-value gain — and says how much of the headline it explains.

04

What would prove it wrong

The swing assumption is stated, along with the evidence that would change the call. Estimates marked EQe are Equinox’s own; the rest is sourced on the page.
