Glass Wall Systems

H2 is in the order book; FY28 needs US orders back

Glass Wall Systems builds façades in India and exports them, mainly to the US. Q2 FY27 PAT rose 31% y/y to ₹28.6 cr on revenue of ₹131.5 cr (+3%), helped by two items. EBITDA of ₹37.5 cr (28.5% margin) included ₹6 cr of escalation claims for costs booked earlier; without them the margin was 25.1%. Revenue included ₹9 cr of export freight now billed to customers; net of it, sales fell 4% in Q2 and rose 9% in H1. Guidance was called unchanged, yet ‘around 25%’ growth on the October call became 20-22% net of freight in November.

H2 should be strong as three export projects ship. We forecast H2 revenue of ₹340 cr (+36%) and FY27E revenue of ₹579 cr, up 18.4% net of freight and just under guidance, at a 24.5% EBITDA margin, for EPS of ₹12.38. FY28 is less visible. The order book slipped from over ₹1,000 cr in June to ₹957 cr; the export book was ₹186 cr in July against FY26 export revenue of ₹207 cr, and US developers are deferring awards. Without new export orders we estimate only ₹30-60 cr of that book is left by March. We assume ₹420 cr of H2 orders and revenue growth of 18% and 16% in FY28-29E.

The shares, up 34% on the ₹182 IPO price, trade at 19.7x FY27E and 17.5x FY28E EPS; four listed building peers with c.10% EBITDA margins trade at a 13x forward median. Our Dec-27 target of ₹255 averages 16x forward EPS (midpoint of today’s 18x and peers) and an FCFE DCF of ₹256 (12% cost of equity, 5.5% terminal growth). No debt, ₹114 cr of net cash and a 39% FY26 ROE support the premium. We rate the shares Neutral (ladder: −5% to +15%). Upside risk: US awards resume. Downside risks: claims-led margins, share supply (a pre-IPO fund trust owns 9.4%, AIFs 12.9%; the IPO was 86% offer for sale) and a ₹33 cr sales-tax demand before the Bombay High Court.
