The core compounds; InstaHelp spends it

Urban Company’s second quarter split in two. Net transaction value (NTV) rose 39% to ₹1,432cr and revenue 41% to ₹537cr. Excluding InstaHelp, adjusted EBITDA tripled to ₹30cr. InstaHelp, the on-demand domestic-help service, lost ₹174cr on ₹21cr of revenue, taking the group to −₹144cr and a net loss of ₹182cr. A QIP of up to ₹2,500cr opened on 16 November at a ₹161 floor, on top of ₹1,901cr of cash. Management kept both targets: breakeven by Q3 FY28 and ₹1,000cr of adjusted EBITDA by FY31. The shares are 29% higher this year and 67% above the ₹103 IPO price.

1) FY27 revenue is ₹2,231cr (+43%) on NTV of ₹6,051cr; the India core grows 28%. 2) InstaHelp loses ₹736cr in FY27 and ₹660cr in FY28: loss per order fell from ₹346 to ₹279, but orders rose 63% and the price per order fell 17%. 3) Ex-InstaHelp EBITDA doubles to ₹207cr, then ₹361cr. Breakeven comes in Q1 FY29, two quarters after guidance; FY31 EBITDA is ₹1,018cr against the ₹1,000cr target. 4) EPS is −₹3.94, −₹1.79 and ₹1.32. Adjusted EBITDA excludes share-based pay, ₹56cr last quarter or 10% of revenue.

Our Dec-27 target of ₹165 averages two methods that agree. At 7x Dec-28E sales of ₹3,574cr — today’s 8.3x on FY28E, scaled down as growth slows from 33% to 28% — plus ₹4,049cr of cash, the shares are worth ₹171. At 30x FY31E EBITDA, discounted at 12%, they are worth ₹163; enterprise value today is already 24x that EBITDA. Nine brokers have a mean target of ₹158; Morgan Stanley is at ₹190 and Goldman Sachs at ₹130. We rate the shares Neutral (band: −5% to +15%). Upside risks: InstaHelp pricing recovers, the core reaches its 9-10% margin early, Native air conditioners. Downside: a longer price war with Snabbit and Pronto, more dilution, gig-worker regulation.
