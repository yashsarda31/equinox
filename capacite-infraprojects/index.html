Capacite Infraprojects (₹171.85, 18 Nov 26 close) trades at 7.5x FY26 EPS of ₹22.82 and 0.73x book, -42% below its 52-week high. Q2 revenue rose 19% to a record ₹770 cr, EBITDA ₹119 cr (15.5%), PAT ₹48 cr, down 7%; H1 revenue ₹1,400 cr, up 13%. Labour normalised. A 16-Nov CIDCO LoA (₹670 cr, MAHA Awas DRS, Belapur; 42 months) lifts FY27 inflows to ₹3,157 cr vs a ₹4,500-5,000 cr target. The price discounts MSKA’s qualified conclusion — the same ₹11.6 cr CIRP receivable, qualified at least a fourth period running — plus an unmodified ₹54.9 cr emphasis of matter on legal enforcement, and cash plus bank balances of ₹22 cr.

1) H2 FY27E: revenue ₹1,595 cr, up 15% on H2 FY26; FY27E revenue ₹2,995 cr (+14%) with EBITDA margin 15.7%; EPS ₹22.99, 6% below consensus; consensus needs H2 profit up 26%. 2) FY28E/FY29E revenue ₹3,385/3,835 cr (+13%/+13%), margins back to 16.0%/16.2% as commodity headwinds ease (Vision 2028: >₹4,000 cr, 16-17% margin by FY28); EPS ₹27.70 and ₹33.48 (+20%/+21%). 3) The order book is ₹13,013 cr (standalone), 4.7x book-to-bill, 54% public sector, 62% above 40 floors. Working-capital days fell 43 days in FY26 (+11 vs Q1 FY27); FY26 cash PAT ₹318 cr (as reported).

Our Dec-27 target of ₹340 averages the peer-median forward P/E (12.0x, ex-RPP) on twelve months after Dec-27 — ₹32.04 EPS gives ₹384 — with a ten-year FCFE DCF at 14% and 5.5% terminal growth, ₹289. Own three-year average P/E, 12.5x, would give ₹400; the consensus mean is ₹272, high ₹273 (2 brokers). The price implies 6.7x next-twelve-months. What would prove us wrong: another qualified period, FY27 inflows missing ₹4,500 cr, EBITDA margin failing to hold near 16%, or the CIRP receivable failing. Cash conversion is the swing factor — H1 operating cash ₹73 cr against ₹87 cr PAT.
