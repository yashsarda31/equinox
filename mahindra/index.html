An incentive in the profit, a discount in the price

M&M has risen 13% since its Q2 FY27 results on 5 November (NIFTY: 5%). Consolidated net profit grew 25% to ₹4,588 cr. Two facts sit behind that. On the call the CFO said about ₹1,000 cr of production-linked incentive (PLI) was booked in the quarter: after tax, some 15% of net profit, and more than the electric-SUV segment’s ₹624 cr profit. And core margins fell: SUVs and pickups earned 9.8% (11.0% a year ago), tractors 17.6% (20.6%). Standalone EBITDA before investment income rose 3% in H1 on revenue up 25%.

1) SUV volumes grow 15% in FY27E (guidance: mid-to-high teens); H2 needs 67,000 a month against capacity of 68,000, rising to 82,000 by March. 2) Tractor volumes fall 9% in H2: the monsoon was 87% of normal, October domestic sales fell 13%, and management sees a flat year. 3) Margins recover part of the loss in FY28E as 4-5% price rises meet easing costs. Standalone EPS of ₹138 (FY27E) and ₹159 (FY28E) is 2% and 4% above consensus. 4) The PLI scheme covers sales to FY28. We assume it ends and cost and pricing replace half: consolidated EPS grows 15% a year to FY28E, then 1%.

Our Dec-27 target of ₹3,850 is a sum of parts on earnings 12 months ahead. Auto and Farm at 21.5x, the median forward P/E of nine listed Indian vehicle makers, are worth ₹3,226 a share. Tech Mahindra and Mahindra Finance at market value less 20% add ₹497; the smaller businesses, at 18x less 20%, ₹123. Net cash of ₹44,685 cr is not added: its income is inside segment profit. If PLI is extended the target is ₹4,240; if it ends with nothing replaced, ₹3,560. Even that is above the price, which values Auto and Farm at 18x. Consensus target: ₹3,977. Downside: a second weak monsoon; capex (H1: ₹7,365 cr, ₹3,957 cr a year ago) running ahead of demand.
