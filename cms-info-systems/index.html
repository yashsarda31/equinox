Services are at a record; the shares are near a low

CMS Info Systems posted a fourth straight record for services revenue in the September quarter: ₹644cr, up 16.2%. Total revenue rose only 6.7% to ₹650cr because product sales fell to ₹6cr. EBITDA rose 24.8% to ₹173cr and the margin widened 390bp to 26.7%, but profit rose just 10.3% to ₹81cr as depreciation climbed 52% to ₹73cr. Technology and payments reached ₹127cr, 20% of services. Management kept the FY27 guidance it reset in August: services of ₹2,650-2,750cr at a 27% margin. Operating cash flow was ₹177cr in the half and ₹168cr went on a buyback at ₹340. The shares are 39% lower this year and 4% above the ₹197 low; FIIs own 19.8%, against 37.8% in Mar-25.

1) FY27 revenue is ₹2,686cr (+8%), with services of ₹2,611cr, 1.5% below the guided low end: the second half needs services up 13% while public-sector repricing still sits with the banks’ association. 2) EBITDA margin rises from 24.1% to 26.7%, then 26.8% and 27.0%. 3) Depreciation peaks at ₹300cr, 11% of revenue, so profit grows 10% to ₹335cr against Jefferies’ ₹355cr. 4) EPS is ₹20.76, ₹23.01 and ₹26.22, from ₹18.26 in FY26.

Our Dec-27 target of ₹280 averages two methods that agree. At 11x Dec-28E EPS of ₹25.41, a turn above today’s 9.9x FY27E and below the 12x and 14x that Jefferies and Kotak use, the shares are worth ₹280. At 5x Dec-28E EBITDA of ₹850cr, against 4.5x today, plus ₹299cr of cash net of leases, they are worth ₹284. The price already discounts our bear case of ₹199. Three brokers average ₹285. We rate the shares Overweight (band: above +15%). Upside risks: public-sector repricing, recycler deliveries, HAWKAI mandates. Downside: currency supply, off-site ATM closures, wage inflation, further FII selling.
