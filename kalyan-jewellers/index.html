Growth intact, margins on watch

Kalyan reports Q2 FY27 today. The 7 October update already gave consolidated revenue growth ‘in excess of 26%’ (India c.+27%, same-store sales c.+20% on a Navratri-heavy base, Candere c.+64%), so the print is about margins, not sales. We forecast revenue of ₹9,977 cr, EBITDA of ₹609 cr (6.1% margin vs. 6.3% a year ago) and PAT of ₹330 cr (+26% y/y), a little under press previews of c.⁠₹340 cr: management is steering recycled gold to 55-60% of sales and puts the dilution from exchange at 20-30bp. Its pledge to hold last year’s PBT margin in FY27 needs H2 to deliver, with Q1 at 4.4% against 5.0% in FY26; we model 4.9% and sit 1-3% above consensus EPS for FY27-28E.

The longer case rests on three things we can measure. 1) Network: 546 showrooms at end-September, with 84 Kalyan and 50 Candere openings targeted for FY27, largely franchisee-funded (FOCO stores were 234 of 354 in India at June). 2) Balance sheet: non-GML debt reached zero in Q2, ₹102 cr of non-core land is being sold and India Ratings has the company at AA-/Positive. 3) Returns: company-defined ROCE rose from 19.8% in FY25 to 28.8% in FY26. We forecast a 22% EPS CAGR over FY26-29E.

The shares are not cheap at 38x FY27E and 31x FY28E EPS. Our Dec-27 price target of ₹730 applies 32x to twelve-month forward EPS at that date, in line with the 33x paid today, so the 19% upside is earnings growth, not re-rating. That multiple needs a long runway: a ten-year FCFE DCF (12% cost of equity, 5.5% terminal growth) gives only ₹373, and today’s price implies c.23% annual profit growth over FY30-36. We rate the shares Overweight (our ladder: >15% upside). Key risks are a sharp gold-price fall or duty cut, lasting exchange-led margin dilution and a slower franchise roll-out.
